Our Solution

The future of fall prevention

Dynamic perturbation training exposes people to small, safe, controlled slips so their bodies learn to recover.

  • Improve reaction time and stability
  • Prevent falls

How the Surefooted Trainer works

  1. WalkUsers walk back and forth across the trainer.
  2. SlipRollers are locked or released with a brake to create a sudden, safe slip.
  3. AdaptUsers adapt to the slips rapidly, building the reflexes that prevent real-world falls.
